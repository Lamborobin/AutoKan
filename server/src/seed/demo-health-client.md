# Client: Norvik Health

Norvik Health is a regional public hospital network in northern Europe operating three sites — a central acute hospital, a community hospital, and an outpatient and rehabilitation centre. It serves a catchment of roughly 480,000 people across emergency, medical, surgical, maternity, paediatric, and outpatient services.

## Organisation

- ~6,200 staff across clinical, nursing, allied health, pharmacy, and administration
- ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management) certified; accredited by the national healthcare inspectorate
- Electronic health record platform: CareView EHR (clinical documentation, e-prescribing, results)
- Sites: Central Acute Hospital (≈540 beds), Community Hospital (≈180 beds), Outpatient & Rehabilitation Centre
- Patient data is governed under GDPR — documents and templates must never contain real patient-identifiable information

## Key Stakeholders

- **Medical Director** — executive clinical lead; final clinical authority for organisation-wide protocols
- **Director of Nursing** — owns nursing standards, care documentation, and ward-level procedures
- **Clinical Governance Lead** — owns clinical policy, document control, and audit; signs off clinical protocols
- **Patient Safety Officer** — owns incident reporting, root cause analysis, and safety alerts
- **Data Protection Officer** — signs off anything touching patient-identifiable information or data handling

## Communication Style

Precise, evidence-based, and patient-safety-first. Norvik values clarity and traceability — every clinical document must be unambiguous, reference the relevant clinical guideline or standard, and name the responsible clinical role. Patient-facing material must be in plain, reassuring language a non-clinician can act on. Avoid vague language in any clinical instruction. Clinical terminology is expected and appropriate in staff-facing sections.
