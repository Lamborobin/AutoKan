# Project: Risk, Credit & Compliance Documentation

This board manages policy, risk, and compliance documentation across Meridian Capital's lending and investment arms.

## Scope

- Credit and lending policies and procedures — drafting, review, and approval workflows
- AML / KYC procedures and customer due-diligence documentation
- Risk assessment frameworks and control documentation
- Investment process, suitability, and product / risk disclosures (customer-facing)
- Regulatory compliance documentation and audit / examination preparation

## Document Standards

All documents must follow Meridian's document standard. Required sections in order:

1. **Purpose** — what this document covers and why it exists
2. **Scope** — which products, customers, or staff it applies to
3. **Responsibilities** — named roles (not generic "staff") with specific obligations
4. **Procedure** — numbered step-by-step instructions
5. **Risk & Controls** — ⚠️ prefix on any risk- or compliance-critical step; the control and who performs it
6. **Regulatory References** — the specific regulation(s), directive(s), or internal policy this implements
7. **Approval & Review** — version number, effective date, document owner, named sign-off role, and next review date

Additional rules:
- Plain, actionable language in any customer-facing section — no unexplained financial jargon
- Risk-/compliance-critical steps marked with ⚠️ — credit decisions, AML/KYC checks, suitability assessments, limit breaches, customer-money handling
- Version number, effective date, and review date in the document header
- All roles referenced by job title, not by a person's name
- No real customer-identifiable or account data anywhere — use placeholders (e.g. [CUSTOMER NAME], [ACCOUNT NUMBER])
- Financial figures, interest rates, fees, limits, and thresholds must come from a cited source — never invented

## Verification Checklist (Compliance Reviewer agent)

When verifying any document submitted to this board, check ALL of the following and report PASS or FAIL with a brief note on each:

1. All seven required sections present and in correct order
2. Risk-/compliance-critical steps marked with ⚠️
3. Each control names the responsible role and what is checked
4. Version number, effective date, and review date present in the document header
5. Responsibilities section lists specific job titles, not generic terms
6. Procedure section uses numbered steps, not bullet points
7. Regulatory References section cites at least one specific regulation, directive, or internal policy
8. No real customer-identifiable or account data present — placeholders only
9. No invented figures, rates, fees, limits, or thresholds — anything quantitative is sourced or flagged
10. No ambiguous language ("as needed", "occasionally", "may") in risk or procedure sections

If any check FAILS, list exactly what needs to be corrected. Do not approve a document with any failing checks. Final sign-off is the Head of Compliance's decision — this agent reports readiness, it does not grant approval.
