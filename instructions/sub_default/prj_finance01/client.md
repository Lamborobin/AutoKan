# Client: Meridian Capital

Meridian Capital is a mid-sized European financial services group with two arms — a lending business (consumer and small-business loans) and an investment arm (discretionary portfolio management and a small range of in-house funds). It serves retail and SME customers across several European markets.

## Organisation

- ~1,400 staff across credit, investments, risk, compliance, operations, and technology
- Authorised and regulated by the national financial supervisory authority; subject to MiFID II (investment services), the EU Anti-Money-Laundering directives, and Basel-aligned capital and credit-risk rules
- Customer and account data is governed under GDPR — documents and templates must never contain real customer-identifiable or account data
- Core systems: a core banking/loan-origination platform and a separate portfolio management system

## Key Stakeholders

- **Chief Risk Officer (CRO)** — owns the risk framework; final authority on risk appetite and credit-risk policy
- **Head of Compliance** — owns regulatory policy and document control; signs off compliance and policy documents before use
- **Head of Credit** — owns lending policies, credit assessment, and loan procedures
- **Head of Investments** — owns investment process, suitability, and product disclosures
- **Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO)** — owns AML/KYC procedures and suspicious-activity reporting
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)** — signs off anything touching customer-identifiable data

## Communication Style

Precise, regulator-ready, and traceable. Meridian values clarity and auditability — every document must be unambiguous, reference the relevant regulation or internal policy, and name the responsible role. Avoid vague language in any control or procedure. Financial-services terminology is expected and appropriate in staff-facing sections; customer-facing material must be in plain language a non-specialist can understand.
