# Client: Nordstahl Industries

Nordstahl Industries is a mid-sized European steel manufacturer operating three production facilities — a primary steel mill, a cold-rolling plant, and a finishing and distribution centre. They produce structural steel, flat-rolled sheet, and specialty alloy components serving the construction, automotive, and industrial equipment sectors.

## Organisation

- ~850 employees across production, quality, maintenance, logistics, and administration
- ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management) and ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health & Safety) certified
- ERP platform: SAP S/4HANA for production planning, inventory, and procurement
- Primary sites: Germany (headquarters + primary mill), Poland (cold-rolling), Netherlands (finishing + distribution)

## Key Stakeholders

- **Operations Director** — executive sponsor; approves all operational changes
- **Quality Manager** — owns compliance documentation, internal audits, and certification renewals
- **Maintenance Supervisor** — manages planned preventive maintenance and reactive breakdown response
- **HSE Coordinator** — Health, Safety & Environment; signs off on all safety-critical SOPs

## Communication Style

Direct and precise. Nordstahl values clarity and traceability — every document must be unambiguous and reference the relevant standard or regulation where applicable. Avoid vague language; prefer specific, measurable statements. Steel industry terminology is expected and appropriate.
