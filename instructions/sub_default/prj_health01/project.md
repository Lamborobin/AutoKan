# Project: Clinical Documentation & Patient Safety

This board manages clinical documentation, patient-safety workflows, and care-process improvement for Norvik Health across its three sites.

## Scope

- Clinical protocols and care pathways — drafting, review, and approval workflows
- Patient information leaflets and discharge instructions — plain-language, patient-facing
- Standard Operating Procedures for clinical and ward processes
- Patient-safety incident documentation and root cause analysis reports
- Clinical audit preparation and ISO 9001 / inspectorate compliance tracking

## Document Standards

All documents must follow Norvik's clinical document standard. Required sections in order:

1. **Purpose** — what this document covers and why it exists
2. **Scope** — which patients, departments, or staff it applies to
3. **Responsibilities** — named clinical roles (not generic "staff") with specific obligations
4. **Procedure** — numbered step-by-step clinical or process instructions
5. **Safety & Escalation** — ⚠️ prefix on any safety-critical step; how and to whom to escalate a deteriorating or at-risk patient
6. **Patient Information** — plain-language guidance where the document is patient-facing (omit if staff-only, but state that it was considered)
7. **References** — relevant clinical guidelines, standards, or parent policies
8. **Approval & Review** — version number, effective date, author, named clinical sign-off, and next review date

Additional rules:
- Plain, actionable language in any patient-facing section — no unexplained clinical jargon
- Safety-critical steps marked with ⚠️ — medication, allergy checks, escalation of a deteriorating patient, infection control, invasive procedures
- Version number, effective date, and review date in the document header
- All roles referenced by job title, not by a person's name
- No real patient-identifiable information anywhere — use placeholders (e.g. [PATIENT NAME], [NHS/ID NUMBER])
- Clinical facts, drug doses, and vital-sign thresholds must come from a cited source — never invented

## Verification Checklist (Clinical Governance Checker agent)

When verifying any document submitted to this board, check ALL of the following and report PASS or FAIL with a brief note on each:

1. All eight required sections present and in correct order
2. Safety-critical steps marked with ⚠️
3. Escalation path is explicit — names the role to escalate to and the trigger
4. Version number, effective date, and review date present in the document header
5. Responsibilities section lists specific job titles, not generic terms
6. Procedure section uses numbered steps, not bullet points
7. References section cites at least one clinical guideline, standard, or parent policy
8. No real patient-identifiable information present — placeholders only
9. No invented clinical facts, drug doses, or thresholds — anything clinical is sourced or flagged
10. No ambiguous language ("as needed", "occasionally", "may") in safety or procedure sections

If any check FAILS, list exactly what needs to be corrected. Do not approve a document with any failing checks. Final clinical approval is the Clinical Governance Lead's decision — this agent reports readiness, it does not grant clinical sign-off.
